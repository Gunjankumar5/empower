Manual Tag Registration

If your browser does not support Web NFC, paste the tag ID here and save it to your profile.

How it works

Your tag is tied to your profile. If someone scans it, the app can trigger a safety alert using your last known location.

Web NFC is not supported in this browser. Use Android Chrome with NFC enabled to scan a tag.
  1. Tap Scan NFC Tag.
  2. Bring the tag close to the device.
  3. The tag ID is saved to your profile.
