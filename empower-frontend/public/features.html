Link Tag to Account
Scan your NFC tag to link it to your profile so the backend knows whose SOS to trigger when someone taps it.

💡 Tip: First program your tag in the Setup tab, then come here to link it to your account.

⚡ How to Set Up (2 Steps)

1
Go to Setup tab → click "Program NFC Tag Now" → hold sticker to TOP BACK of your S24
2
Go to Link Tag tab → click "Scan NFC Tag to Link" → tap sticker again
✅
Done! Anyone tapping the sticker anywhere triggers your SOS + SMS instantly

📍 Best Places to Put Your Tag

👜
Inside handbag flap — most accessible in emergency
📱
Back of phone case — always with you
🪪
Behind ID card in wallet — discreet, always carried
⌚
Watch strap or wristband — hidden and easy to tap

⚠️ Important Rules

❌
Never on metal — blocks NFC completely (laptop, keys, etc.)
🔓
Phone must be unlocked — NFC blocked on lock screen
📱
Android Chrome only — Web NFC not supported on iPhone
📶
NFC must be ON — Settings → Connections → NFC → toggle ON
